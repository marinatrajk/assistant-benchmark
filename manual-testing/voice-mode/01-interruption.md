# V01 — Interruption and changed instruction

**Purpose:** Can the assistant stop speaking and follow a changed request?

## Steps

1. In a fresh voice conversation, say: **“Explain how photosynthesis works. Give me a detailed explanation that takes about one minute.”**
2. After five seconds of substantive assistant speech, interrupt at normal speaking volume: **“Actually, explain it in one sentence for a ten-year-old.”** Do not use a stop button or mute control.
3. Let the new response finish. Record any repetition needed.

## Pass criteria

- The original assistant speech ends within **two seconds of the start of your interruption**.
- The replacement explanation is one sentence in accessible language and correctly describes plants using sunlight to make food from water and carbon dioxide. Mentioning oxygen is optional.
- The assistant does not resume the abandoned long explanation.

**Partial:** It follows the correction but stops more than two seconds after interruption, needs a repeated interruption, or the timing evidence is insufficient. A short acknowledgment is not counted as an extra explanation sentence.

**Failed:** It continues the original explanation without incorporating the interruption, gives another multi-sentence explanation, or gives an incorrect explanation after the correction. If it finishes before the planned interruption, record an invalid attempt and repeat with a new ID.

**Evidence:** Audio/video containing both voices; interruption start, original speech end, replacement answer and any retries. Record interruption latency separately from the new response's latency.
