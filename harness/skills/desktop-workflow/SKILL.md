---
name: desktop-workflow
description: Complete a small macOS task by observing, acting, and verifying the visible result.
---
1. Take a computer_snapshot to inspect the current primary display.
2. Open the target application with computer_open.
3. Work only in the requested app. Inspect screenshots before choosing coordinates.
4. Use short key sequences and verify the visible outcome after actions.
5. Report the observed result. Do not claim success from mental calculation alone.
6. If macOS permissions prevent access, explain the required permission and stop.
